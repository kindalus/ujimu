Slice 9

Question analytics & content gaps

Record questions by specialist and surface repeated themes that may justify new wiki content.

question logs content gaps 50 visitors/month not a source
Idea

User questions are editorial signals.

Repeated questions show what the public needs, but analytics must never become an uncited source for answers.

Capture

Log enough to see patterns.

  • Raw question text with a defensive length limit.
  • Normalized text and fingerprint for deterministic grouping.
  • Specialist ID, timestamp, timezone, visitor identity, optional user/conversation reference.
  • Only visible outcomes: answered or insufficient context.
Visitor metric

MVP success uses combined distinct-visitor signals.

The target is 50 distinct monthly visitors, measured with a first-party visitor cookie and authenticated user IDs. Count user ID first; otherwise count the visitor cookie.

Aggregation

Repeated questions become gap candidates.

  • Group by specialist first.
  • Group by specialist and normalized question fingerprint.
  • MVP threshold: at least 2 occurrences in the last 30 days.
  • Highlight insufficient-context repeats as stronger wiki-gap signals.
Dashboard

The admin view should answer editorial questions.

What is asked often?Top repeated questions per specialist.
Where does the wiki fail?Questions that returned insufficient context.
What is growing?Trends by week and month.
What should be curated?Candidate topics for new wiki pages or sources.
Review lifecycle

Reviewed does not mean forgotten.

  • Admins can mark a candidate as reviewed.
  • Reviewed candidates are hidden from the current list.
  • If the same fingerprint crosses the threshold again after review, it resurfaces.
  • No comments, assignments, or auto-wiki creation in this slice.
Privacy

Conversation deletion removes readable analytics.

  • Deleting a conversation removes or anonymizes linked raw question logs.
  • Successful question edits keep the old analytics event.
  • Aggregate anonymous counters may remain after deletion.
  • Analytics stores no answer text, document contents, OTPs, prompts, or secrets.
Boundary

Analytics is not answer grounding.

Question logs can suggest new content work, but user answers still require wiki/source citations.

Success conditions

What must be true when the slice is done.

  • Only answered and insufficient-context visible outcomes are recorded.
  • Admins can see repeated-question candidates per specialist.
  • Reviewed candidates hide until renewed recurrence after review.
  • Distinct monthly visitors are counted from first-party visitor/user identities.
  • No answer uses analytics data as a citation or factual source.
